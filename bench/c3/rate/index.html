C3 blind design rating

Thanks for rating. This takes about 15 minutes.

  1. Check the issues labelled c3-rating and pick a packet (P1 to P6) with fewer than three submissions.
  2. You must not have built any of these pages.
  3. For each item, judge only which page looks more like the original's design direction for the task. Do not judge which you like more, or which is more complete.
  4. Submit one C3 rating issue with your packet and ten picks. Rate once.
