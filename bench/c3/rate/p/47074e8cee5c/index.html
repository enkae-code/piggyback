What’s growing, season by season

Small on purpose

Twenty-two beds, one greenhouse, and two of us most days. We keep the farm small so every bed gets looked at, by hand, every morning.

  1. No sprays, ever

    No synthetic pesticides or herbicides. We use insect netting and companion flowers, and pick off pests by hand.

  2. Feed the soil, not the plant

    Beds are never tilled. Each season they get a layer of compost made on site from our scraps and the café next door’s coffee grounds.

  3. Rain first, tap second

    Two 1,000-litre tanks catch the roof runoff, and drip lines carry it to the roots. In a dry August, that covers about two-thirds of our watering.

  4. Picked the morning you buy it

    Nothing sits in a cooler for a week. Harvest starts at 6 a.m., and boxes are packed by 9.

The Saturday market

Every Saturday we set out trestle tables at the farm gate. You can bring a bag, pick from the crates and talk to whoever grew it.

Winter hours start 6 December. We close for two weeks over the holidays, 20 December to 3 January.
