How we grow

Small beds, slow soil, no shortcuts.

We farm the way a careful gardener would — just a little more of it. No sprays, no peat, no digging the life out of the ground.

¼acre of beds
60+varieties a year
0miles by van

  1. No-dig beds

    Compost goes on top each winter and the worms do the rest, keeping the soil structure and its carbon in place.

  2. City compost

    Coffee grounds from three cafés and leaf litter from the park become next year’s growing medium.

  3. Rain, not mains

    Four rooftop tanks catch enough water to carry the beds through most summers.

  4. Saved seed

    We keep seed from our best plants each autumn, so the varieties slowly learn this corner of town.
