01 · The day

One afternoon, everybody teaches something.

Good Day Assembly is a free, open-air swap where neighbors trade skills instead of money. Fix a bike, learn a stitch, pot a cutting, show someone how you make your bread. Nobody has to be an expert — just willing.

Drop in for an hour or stay all day. Kids, grandparents, and the curious are all welcome.

02 · Make, trade, play

Three things to do.

Each runs on a loose rotation all afternoon. Bring a skill, leave with another one.

  • Make11:00 – 2:00

    Fix-It Tables

    Bring a wobbly chair, a flat tire, or a toaster with opinions. Neighbors with tools and patience help you mend it — and show you how.

    Bring: something broken

  • Trade12:00 – 4:00

    The Skills Market

    Fifteen-minute lessons, traded one for one. Knot tying for knife sharpening, Spanish phrases for sourdough tips. Chalk your offer on the board.

    Bring: one thing you know

  • Play2:00 – 5:00

    Lopsided Workshop

    Clay, scrap wood, and fabric off-cuts for making something gloriously imperfect. Then a slow-paced field-day relay to close out the afternoon.

    Bring: clothes you can get messy

03 · Getting there

Everyone gets in.

If something here doesn't work for you, tell us when you save your place and we'll sort it out ahead of time.

  • Step-freePaved paths from the Alder St. gate to every table. Firm ground mats on the lawn.
  • TransitBus 14 and 22 stop at Alder & 5th, a two-minute walk. Bike racks at both gates.
  • ParkingSix accessible spaces at the Rec Hall lot, held for blue-badge holders all day.
  • FacilitiesAccessible restrooms in the Rec Hall. Shaded seating and water at every station.
  • Quiet tentA low-noise rest spot by the oak grove, open the whole day.
  • LanguageASL interpreter 12–3 pm. Volunteers speak Spanish and Vietnamese.
