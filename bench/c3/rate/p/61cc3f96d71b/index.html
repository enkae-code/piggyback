Our growing approach

A quarter acre, worked by hand.

Our lot was a parking pad for twenty years. We built the soil up from there, and the plants grow in what we built.

  1. Soil first

    We never till. Compost from our neighbours’ kitchen scraps goes on top of the beds every autumn, and the worms pull it down.

  2. No synthetic sprays

    We use row covers, flowering borders for helpful insects, and rotation instead of pesticides. Sometimes a pest wins and that crop takes a week off.

  3. Rainwater, then drip

    Two cisterns catch water off the warehouse roof next door. Drip lines put it at the roots, so very little is lost to evaporation.

  4. Picked for this week

    We harvest Friday and Saturday morning for the market and boxes. Nothing is held in storage except the squash and garlic, which keep well.
