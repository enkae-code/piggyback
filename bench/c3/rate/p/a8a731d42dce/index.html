Why we exist

Our mission

We turn unused city ground into shared gardens, so that anyone on the block can learn to grow food and nobody on it goes without fresh vegetables.

Little Plot is a registered nonprofit run entirely by volunteers. We keep no private beds and sell nothing. Seeds, tools and water are paid for by small donations and a yearly plant sale, and every decision about what to grow is made at an open meeting on the first Monday of the month.

Produce share

What’s coming out of the ground

Every Sunday we split the harvest three ways: a bag for each volunteer, a box for families on the share list, and the rest to the Alder Street pantry. Here’s what’s in the boxes this month.

  • Salad greens

    Lettuce, arugula and mizuna, cut fresh Sunday morning.

    About 60 bags a week

  • Tomatoes

    Cherry and slicing varieties, saved from our own seed.

    Peak season, ~120 lb

  • Pole beans

    Pick-your-own along the east fence on work days.

    Through September

  • Herbs

    Basil, parsley, dill and mint, bundled for every box.

    Free cuttings at the gate

  1. Sign up once. Add your household at the gate or at any shift. No income check, no fee.
