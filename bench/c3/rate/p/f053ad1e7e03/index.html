Our mission

We turn an empty lot into a volunteer-run garden that grows fresh, chemical-free food, teaches anyone who wants to learn, and gives every harvest back to our neighbours.

No plot fees, no experience needed — just a pair of hands and an hour or two in the week.

Produce share

What’s in the basket this season.

Every Sunday the week’s harvest goes out on long tables by the gate. Take what you’ll use — volunteers, neighbours and the local food pantry all share from the same harvest.

  • Tomatoes

    Heirloom slicers and cherry tomatoes from the south beds.

    July – September

  • Leafy greens

    Chard, kale, lettuce and spinach, cut fresh the morning of the share.

    April – November

  • Roots

    Carrots, beets and new potatoes pulled on Saturday harvest day.

    June – October

  • Herbs & flowers

    Basil, mint, dill and a cut-flower bunch for the kitchen table.

    May – October
