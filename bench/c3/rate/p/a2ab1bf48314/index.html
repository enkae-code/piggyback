How we grow

Small beds, careful hands, and soil we’ve built from scratch on a former parking lot.

  1. Soil first

    We compost the neighbourhood’s food scraps and leaf litter into our beds. No synthetic fertilisers, no pesticides — ever.

  2. No-till beds

    Permanent raised beds keep the soil life intact, hold water through dry summers and lock carbon in the ground.

  3. Picked to order

    We harvest the morning of each market and box day, so food travels a few blocks — not a few hundred miles.
