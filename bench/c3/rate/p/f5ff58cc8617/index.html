    The Tidewater Almanac

    A four-volume natural history of the Chesapeake. The cover uses one engraved line per tide recorded on the day each volume was finished, and the interior is set in a two-column grid so field notes can sit next to the main text.

  2. 02

    A Ledger of Small Mercies

    A debut collection of letters, set to read like the account books they were found in. The cover has a double-rule frame, every opening has hand-drawn running heads, and a stitched binding lets the book lie flat.

    Client
    Fernhouse Editions
    Scope
    Cover, interior, binding
    Year
    2024
  3. 03

    Salt, a Cookbook

    Ninety recipes from a coastal kitchen. The cover is one oversized word in front of a mustard sun, and the inside uses a cooking order with ingredients down the margin, so it still reads with a wet thumb on the page.

    Client
    Low Tide Books
    Scope
    Cover, interior, art direction
    Year
    2023
