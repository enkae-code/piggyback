Why we exist

Our mission

Ninth Row exists to grow fresh, pesticide-free vegetables on Alder Street and give every pound away, so no neighbor has to ride two buses for a tomato. We teach anyone who wants to learn, and we ask nothing in return.

The nearest grocery store with a real produce aisle is two transfers from Alder Street. In 2019 a few neighbors cleared the corner lot, built six beds out of salvaged lumber, and started growing what they couldn’t easily buy.

Today Ninth Row is a registered nonprofit run entirely by volunteers. The harvest goes out with no forms, no income checks, and no questions.

Produce share

Every Saturday, the table is free.

Whatever came in on Friday’s harvest goes out on Saturday morning. Bring a bag, take what your household will eat, and leave the rest for the next neighbor. Here’s roughly what to expect through the year.

  • Spring greens

    April – May

    Lettuce mix, spinach, radishes, scallions, and the first sugar snap peas.

  • Summer fruit

    June – August

    Tomatoes, cucumbers, zucchini, green beans, peppers, and bunches of basil.

  • Autumn roots

    September – October

    Winter squash, carrots, beets, potatoes, kale, and the last green tomatoes.

  • Winter pantry

    November – March

    Stored squash and garlic, dried herbs, and seed packets for your own windowsill.

Pickup

Saturdays, 9 am to 1 pm, at the garden gate, 900 Alder Street. Can’t make it? Text a neighbor’s name to our volunteer line and they can pick up for you.
