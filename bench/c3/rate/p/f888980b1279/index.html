01 — Good soil, shared fairly

Our mission

Little Plot exists to grow fresh, healthy food with our neighbours and share it freely. Volunteers plant, tend and harvest side by side, and every harvest goes back to the people who live here.

Feed the block

Every week, at least half of what we grow is set aside for households that need it, distributed with no forms and no questions.

Teach by doing

Seasoned gardeners pair with newcomers on every shift, so skills like composting, seed saving and pruning stay in the community.

Care for the ground

No synthetic pesticides. We rotate crops, build soil with our own compost and leave room for pollinators along every bed.

03 — Produce share

What the beds give back.

Each Saturday from June to October, the harvest is split into free shares for volunteers and neighbouring households. Sizes vary with the season — here is what a typical bag holds.

  • Greens

    May – October

    Lettuce, chard, kale and a handful of herbs: basil, parsley, dill.

  • Fruiting crops

    July – September

    Tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers, summer squash and green beans.

  • Roots

    June – October

    Carrots, beets, radishes and new potatoes, washed and bundled.

  • Seasonal extras

    When we have them

    Berries, cut flowers, saved seed packets and pumpkins in fall.

  1. Add your household to the share list at the gate or during any shift.
  2. Pick up on Saturdays, 12–2 pm, at the garden's north entrance.
  3. Can't make it? Name a neighbour to collect — or ask about delivery.
