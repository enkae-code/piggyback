  1. 01

    Transit Fare App, Screen Reader Study

    A regional transit authority had passed every automated audit, yet blind riders were still buying paper tickets. I ran twelve in-context sessions with VoiceOver and TalkBack users on real buses and platforms.

    The trouble was not labels but time: a ninety-second purchase window and a QR screen that dimmed mid-scan. We rebuilt the flow around pausing and a persistent ticket.

    Method
    Contextual inquiry, 12 riders
    Duration
    9 weeks
    Outcome
    Task success 41% → 92%

  2. 02

    Public Library Catalogue, Cognitive Access

    A city library network wanted its new catalogue to work for patrons with memory, attention and reading differences. I co-designed with a standing panel of eight adults and two support workers.

    Findings reshaped search: plain-language filters, a visible history of what you tried, and holds that never expire silently. The panel stays on as paid reviewers.

    Method
    Participatory design panel
    Duration
    5 months
    Outcome
    Abandoned searches down 58%

    Library · No. 11

    Show the way back.
    8 panelists2024
  3. 03

    Clinic Booking, Switch & Voice Access

    A network of community clinics saw motor-impaired patients phoning in rather than booking online. I benchmarked switch, voice control and eye-gaze users against the existing calendar widget.

    A single date picker cost switch users over 140 actions. We replaced it with a short list of next-available slots, now the default for every patient.

    Method
    Benchmark & remote observation
    Duration
    7 weeks
    Outcome
    Actions per booking 140 → 18

    Clinic · No. 19

    Count the presses.
