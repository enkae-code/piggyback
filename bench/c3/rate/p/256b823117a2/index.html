The day

One Saturday, one lawn, everyone who lives near it.

Bring one thing you know how to do and leave with something you didn't. Neighbors teach in short, informal sessions. Nobody is paid and nobody is graded.

Make, trade, play

Each one has a neighbor host and runs in 30-minute sessions. Move between them as you like.

Access & getting there

Everyone should be able to get in and get comfortable.

Step-free route

The north lawn is reached from the Alder St entrance on a paved, level path. All three stations sit on firm ground and have table space at seated height.

Transit, bikes & parking

Bus 14 stops at Alder & 9th, two minutes from the entrance. There's bike parking at the gate. Four accessible parking spaces are held on 9th Ave, and there's a drop-off zone right at the entrance.

Restrooms, shade & quiet

The park building has accessible restrooms. There's a shaded rest area with seating and water. For a break from the noise, use the quiet corner under the cedars, away from the stations.

Communication

Hosts wear yellow sashes. Printed large-type schedules are at the welcome table. If you need an ASL interpreter, mention it when you save your place and we'll arrange one.

If it rains: the day moves into the Alder Park community hall next door, which is step-free with an accessible entrance. We'll email everyone who has saved a place by 9 am.
