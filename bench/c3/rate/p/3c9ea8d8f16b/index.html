Before you commit

Fair questions.

When does my first issue arrive?

Subscribe before the 20th of a shipping month and you get that issue. Otherwise your run starts with the next one.

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes. Add the recipient's address at checkout and we'll tuck in a handwritten card instead of a receipt.

Do you ship abroad?

Everywhere Royal Mail reaches. Europe adds £3 per issue, the rest of the world £6, shown before you pay.

What is the riso supplement?

A 24-page companion printed on our own two-drum risograph: outtakes, a short story, and one poster you can pull out.
