Selected case studies, 2023 to 2025

3 of 27

  1. 01

    Renewing a transit pass by voice

    A regional rail operator was about to launch a new app and expected its WCAG audit to pass. Over six weeks I ran sessions with 18 riders who use screen readers, switch access or voice control.

    The audit passed. The renewal flow still failed: a timed payment step and a QR code with no text alternative locked out 11 of the 18 riders. The team rebuilt the step before launch.

    Client
    Regional rail operator
    Method
    Moderated remote sessions
    Outcome
    Renewal success 39% → 94%

  2. 02

    Plain language for a benefits portal

    People with cognitive and learning disabilities were giving up on a public benefits application halfway through. Over eight weeks I ran diary studies and co-design workshops with 14 participants and their support workers.

    We rewrote 42 screens together, split the form into steps you can save, and replaced the jargon with the words participants used themselves.

    Client
    Municipal social services
    Method
    Diary study, co-design
    Outcome
    Abandonment down 58%

  3. 03

    Captions people trust in a learning platform

    An online course provider leaned on auto-captions and assumed they were good enough. I ran comparative sessions with 22 Deaf and hard-of-hearing learners across three subject areas.

    Errors in technical terms led learners to stop trusting all the captions. We set up a glossary-backed review workflow and a way for learners to report caption errors from the player.

    Client
    Online course provider
    Method
    Comparative evaluation
    Outcome
    Completion up 27%

How I work

  • Paid, recruited participants

    Every participant is disabled, recruited through community partners and paid at a professional rate, whether or not their session goes to plan.

  • Audits are where I start

    I check against WCAG 2.2, then watch real use. Most of the barriers that matter show up in the gap between the two.

  • Findings your team can ship

    You get a ranked list of fixes, clips shared with consent, and a working session with the engineers who will make the changes.
