# Volunteer for the C3 test: write a DESIGN.md

## What the test is and why

Piggyback reads a web page and writes a short brief of its measured design: colours, fonts, spacing and layout. C3 compares three ways of telling an AI what a design should look like: the Piggyback brief, a screenshot, and a DESIGN.md that a person wrote by hand. Piggyback may lose. We will publish the results either way, as we did for C1.

## Writers: one page, about 15 minutes

1. Pick one or more starters from the table below.
2. Open the live link and look at the page.
3. Write a DESIGN.md that describes its look well enough that another designer could rebuild the feel. Suggested headings: Colours, Type, Spacing and layout, Components (navigation, buttons, cards), Overall mood. Any length is fine.
4. Submit it with the [C3 DESIGN.md issue form](https://github.com/enkae-code/piggyback/issues/new?template=c3-design-md.yml).

Rules:

- Write it yourself. Do not use AI tools of any kind to draft, edit or check it.
- Do not run Piggyback, and do not read any Piggyback brief for the starter you write about.
- Do not sign up as a rater later.
- One writer per starter. The first complete submission for a starter is used. Later ones are thanked and kept unused, so check the open issues labelled `c3-design-md` before you start. If your starter already has one, pick another.
- Submissions are public on GitHub. Do not include personal information.
- Your text is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](../../starters/LICENSE), the same as the starters.

## Raters: later

A rating round comes after the pages are built. Writers cannot join it.
